
# Notes

## Assumptions
- Contributions are made in GHS.
- Each member contributes a fixed amount per week.
- Partial payments are allowed, but overpayments are rejected.
- A week closes only when all members have paid in full.
- Payouts follow member join order.
- Membership is fixed after the first week closes.
